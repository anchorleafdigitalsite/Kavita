Privacy Policy for Kavita This Privacy Policy explains how Kavita ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our music application. 1. Introduction Kavita is an offline music player that allows you to discover, stream, download, and listen to music on your device. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. 2. Information We Collect Depending on how you use Kavita, we may process the following information: 2.1 Information stored on your device Playlists, favorites, listening history, and app settings Downloaded / offline music files you choose to save Local database and cache used to improve playback performance Optional Last.fm credentials if you enable scrobbling Backups you create or export yourself (e.g. JSON / playlist files) This data stays on your device unless you choose to export, share, or back it up yourself. 2.2 Permissions we may request Internet – to stream music, load content, and show ads Audio / storage access – to play and manage local or downloaded music Notifications – to show playback controls and media notifications Wake lock / foreground service – to keep playback running in the background Advertising ID (AD_ID) – used by advertising partners for personalized or non-personalized ads 2.3 Automatically collected technical data (via ads / SDKs) When ads are shown, third-party advertising SDKs (such as Unity LevelPlay / ironSource and their mediation partners, including Unity Ads and Meta Audience Network) may collect device and advertising-related information, which may include: Device model, OS version, language, and app version IP address and approximate network information Advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID) Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, completions) 3. How We Use Information Provide music playback, offline downloads, and queue features Remember your preferences and library Display advertisements that help keep the App free Improve stability, performance, and user experience Support optional features such as Last.fm scrobbling when enabled Comply with legal obligations where required 4. Advertising Kavita may display banner, interstitial, native, and rewarded ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and related ad networks. These partners may use cookies, advertising IDs, and similar technologies to deliver and measure ads. You can limit personalized advertising in your device settings (for example, "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android). For more information about Unity LevelPlay / ironSource privacy practices, please review their privacy documentation on their official website. 5. Data Sharing We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data only with: Advertising partners – to serve and measure ads Service providers / SDKs – needed to operate streaming, media, or related features Last.fm – only if you choose to connect your account Authorities – if required by law 6.
