Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kavita handles information when you use our manga discovery and reading application.

Information We Collect

Kavita does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, or payment information.

The app may store basic app preferences and saved shelf or favorite items locally on your device so the app can remember your reading choices.

Network Content

Kavita uses internet access to load manga catalog information, covers, chapters, and related metadata from MangaDex services. Requests made to MangaDex may include standard technical information such as your IP address, device type, app request details, and timestamps, as handled by MangaDex.

Advertising

Kavita uses AppLovin MAX to show ads, including interstitial, rewarded, and banner ads. AppLovin and its advertising partners may collect or process device and advertising information to deliver, measure, and improve ads. This may include advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interaction data, diagnostics, and similar technical data.

You can learn more about AppLovin privacy practices at https://www.applovin.com/privacy/.

How We Use Information

  • To display manga catalog content and chapter pages.
  • To remember saved shelf or favorite items on your device.
  • To provide, limit, measure, and improve advertising.
  • To maintain app performance, security, and reliability.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information that we directly collect from you. Third-party services used by the app, including MangaDex and AppLovin, may process data according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Kavita is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Your Choices

  • You can clear local app data from your device settings.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your Android privacy settings.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time to remove locally stored app data.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect app data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
